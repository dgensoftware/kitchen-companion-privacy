Kitchen Companion Cookbook - Privacy Policy

Developer: DGen Software
Effective date: October 14, 2026

Information and Storage

Kitchen Companion Cookbook is designed to store recipes, photographs, bookmarks, and shopping lists locally on your device. DGen Software does not operate developer servers or use advertising, analytics, tracking, or profiling services to collect your personal information.

Internet and Recipe Imports

When you choose to import recipes from websites, the app connects to third-party websites. Those websites may receive information such as your IP address and may use cookies or other technologies according to their own privacy policies. DGen Software does not control those websites.

Google Drive Backup and Restore

Backup and restore are initiated by you using Android's system file picker. You choose where to save or retrieve backup files, including Google Drive if available. DGen Software does not receive your backups or directly access your Google account. Google's handling of files stored in Google Drive is governed by Google's policies.

Data Sharing

DGen Software does not sell or share your recipes or personal information with advertisers or data brokers. Information may be processed by Android, Google, or websites you choose to visit, independently of DGen Software.

Data Deletion

You can delete recipes and other saved information within the app. Uninstalling the app normally removes its locally stored app data. Backup copies saved outside the app must be deleted separately from their storage location.

Children

The app is a general-purpose recipe organizer and is not specifically directed at children.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact dgensoftware@gmail.com. Individual technical support is not guaranteed.
